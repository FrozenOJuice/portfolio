## Problem and solution

Turning scanned multiple-choice exams into grades takes more than recognizing
bubbles. Instructors need to review uncertain answers, make corrections, and
release results to the right students.

Our six-person capstone team built a browser application covering exam setup,
custom answer sheets, PDF uploads, recognition, grading, review, and Canvas release.

## My role

I also worked on exam templates, scanned answer keys, and academic terms.

The recognition engine and complete application were collaborative work.
The [contribution breakdown](https://github.com/FrozenOJuice/omr-project/blob/main/docs/portfolio/contributions.md)

## Technologies and architecture

Angular provides the interface. FastAPI coordinates permissions, exams, grades,
and review states, backed by PostgreSQL and Garage object storage. A separate
Go/OpenCV service handles PDF preprocessing and mark recognition. Docker Compose
connects the services for local development.

## Engineering challenges

### Connecting external records

Canvas courses, rosters, assignments, and grades had to map to the application's
own records. Grade release distinguishes successful, skipped, and failed records
so partial failures can be understood rather than hidden.

### Controlling access to results

Account recovery, administrator actions, and student result links cross frontend,
API, and database boundaries. Canvas tokens are encrypted, and student result
links are time-limited.

### Keeping human review in the workflow

The team kept scanned sheets visible alongside detected responses. Ambiguous marks
remain reviewable, with correction and release history, instead of becoming an
unexplained final score.

## Results, testing, and limitations

The team delivered the exam workflow and completed two rounds of usability testing.
The October 31, 2026 validation records 936 passing frontend tests. OMR checks
passed with two documented VM-dependent CPU tests excluded; the backend suite
was not rerun during that preparation.

[Testing evidence and commands](https://github.com/FrozenOJuice/omr-project/blob/main/docs/portfolio/testing.md)
separate those checks from submission-era results. Live Canvas/email integration
and large-scale deployment were outside that validation.
